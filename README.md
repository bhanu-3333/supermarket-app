# SmartCart - Smart Supermarket Management System

SmartCart is a modern role-based supermarket management system built with React Native (Expo), Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB. It provides a seamless shopping experience by enabling customers to scan products, manage carts, and complete self-checkout while allowing staff to manage inventory and administrators to monitor sales, customers, and stock in real time.

## Problem Statement

Traditional supermarkets rely on manual billing and inventory management, which often leads to long checkout queues, delayed stock updates, and limited visibility into daily business operations. Customers spend unnecessary time waiting at billing counters, while staff and administrators struggle to efficiently monitor inventory and sales.

## Solution

SmartCart digitizes the supermarket workflow through a role-based mobile application.

- **Admin** manages the supermarket, creates staff accounts, monitors inventory, and analyzes sales reports.
- **Staff** scans barcodes, adds new products, and updates inventory in real time.
- **Customers** scan products directly using their mobile device, manage their shopping cart, and complete a faster self-checkout experience.

All data is synchronized through a centralized backend using MongoDB, ensuring inventory, sales, and customer orders remain up to date across all user roles.
